Looking for parts on AliExpress found ESP32 C3 SuperMini, Pushbutton, & Display. I was torn between a 1.28 & 2.1 inch display. a 1.28 inch module would have more bezel % than a 2.1 inch so I went with that.

After finding a good display, I designed a case for my parts. It was finished apart from the holes for buttons.
